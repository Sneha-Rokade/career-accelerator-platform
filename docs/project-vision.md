# PROJECT VISION

## Problem

Many students struggle with:

- Learning path confusion
- Resume building
- Job applications
- Interview preparation

Everything is scattered across different platforms.

---

## Solution

Create a single platform that combines:

- Learning Roadmaps
- Resume Management
- Job Tracking
- DSA Tracking
- AI Guidance

---

## Target Users

1. College Students
2. Freshers
3. Career Switchers
4. Working Professionals

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become a complete career growth platform.
